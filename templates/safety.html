{{ icon('users') }} Shared responsibility

For Donors. For Recipients. For everyone.

{{ icon('check-circle') }} Do

Choose a handover that gives everyone room.

  • {{ icon('check') }} Meet in a public, well-lit place such as a mall lobby or barangay hall.
  • {{ icon('check') }} Tell a friend where you are going and share only a general area first.
  • {{ icon('check') }} Bring a companion for evening pickups or bulky items.
  • {{ icon('check') }} Inspect the item before taking it.
  • {{ icon('check') }} Keep chat and updates inside the Anti-Imbak dashboard where possible.
{{ icon('x-circle') }} Don't

Do not trade away your privacy or safety.

  • {{ icon('close') }} Send passwords, verification codes or banking details.
  • {{ icon('close') }} Pay a deposit to “hold” a free donation.
  • {{ icon('close') }} Meet alone in an isolated location if you feel uncomfortable.
  • {{ icon('close') }} Share your full home address before a claim is accepted.
  • {{ icon('close') }} Ignore threats, harassment or pressure to act quickly.
{{ icon('flag') }} Speak up

Reporting is a community safety tool.

If a listing, message or handover feels unsafe, report it. Reports go privately to the Anti-Imbak team and include the listing, reason and details you provide. The person you report is not told who submitted it.
