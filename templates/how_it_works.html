{{ icon('shield-check') }} Read safety guidelines
Is every item really free? {{ icon('chevron-down') }}

Yes. Anti-Imbak does not allow prices on items. Optional paid services — a featured boost or a delivery arrangement — never change the fact that the donation itself is free.

Why does a claim need a Donor's approval? {{ icon('chevron-down') }}

A donation is a personal handover. The Donor reviews each request, considers the item's fit and chooses the arrangement that feels responsible. Claiming is a request, not an automatic purchase.

How does delivery work? {{ icon('chevron-down') }}

Partnered Delivery is the default when a claim is sent. The fee depends on the item's size and the distance between the Donor and Recipient, stays within ₱50–₱150, and is shown before the request is submitted. The Recipient can choose Self Pickup instead and arrange the meeting with the Donor in Messages.

What information is visible to other members? {{ icon('chevron-down') }}

Your public profile shows your first name, avatar, city and verification status. Your email, contact number and exact handover details are protected and should only be shared once a claim is accepted.

Can I edit or remove a listing? {{ icon('chevron-down') }}

Yes. Donors can edit listings, mark them unavailable and remove them from their dashboard. If a claim has already been accepted, resolve the handover first so the other person is not left without an explanation.

What happens if something feels unsafe? {{ icon('chevron-down') }}

Stop the exchange, leave the situation and use the listing's report button. Reports go privately to the safety team. You can also email hello@antiimbak.ph. In an emergency, contact local authorities first.
