{{ icon('shield-check') }} Read safety guidelines
Is every item really free? {{ icon('chevron-down') }}

Yes. Anti-Imbak does not allow prices on items. Optional paid services — a featured boost or a delivery arrangement — never change the fact that the donation itself is free.

Why does a claim need a Donor's approval? {{ icon('chevron-down') }}

A donation is a personal handover. The Donor reviews each request, considers the item's fit and chooses the arrangement that feels responsible. Claiming is a request, not an automatic purchase.

How does delivery work? {{ icon('chevron-down') }}

Partnered Delivery is the default when a claim is sent. The fee depends on the item's size and distance, stays within ₱50–₱150, and is shown before the request is submitted. You can choose Self Pickup instead and arrange the meeting in Messages.

What happens if something feels unsafe? {{ icon('chevron-down') }}

Stop the exchange, leave the situation and use the listing's report button. Reports go privately to the safety team. You can also email hello@antiimbak.ph. In an emergency, contact local authorities first.
