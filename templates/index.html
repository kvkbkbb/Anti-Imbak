How Anti-Imbak works

No transactions for the items. No price negotiation. Just a thoughtful handover.

01 {{ icon('search') }}

Discover something useful

Browse free donations by category, location and condition. Save the finds that catch your eye.

Start browsing {{ icon('arrow-right') }}
02 {{ icon('message-circle') }}

Tell the Donor why

Send a thoughtful claim request. Partnered Delivery is selected by default, or choose Self Pickup and arrange it in Messages.

03 {{ icon('shield-check') }}

Arrange a safe handover

When accepted, coordinate a public, well-lit meeting point and follow the safety reminders.

04 {{ icon('leaf') }}

Give it a second life

Collect your item, confirm the handover, and keep one more useful thing out of the waste stream.

Why choose Anti-Imbak?

A modern marketplace with a simple rule: the items themselves are never for sale.

{{ icon('gift') }}

Free is not a promotion — it is the point.

Every donation listing stays free. Optional boosts and delivery help sustain the platform without putting a price on generosity.

{{ icon('shield-check') }}

Trust by design

Clear Donor details, verified profiles, reporting tools and safety checkpoints before every finalised handover.

{{ icon('globe') }}

Local by default

Filter by city and condition, then sort by nearest to make pickup practical and low-impact.

{{ icon('refresh') }}

An honest circular loop

Items move from someone who no longer needs them to someone who does — with their history shown clearly.

{{ icon('users') }}

Community-led

Donors review every claim, Recipients share why the item matters, and administrative oversight keeps things fair.

{{ icon('wallet') }}

Transparent revenue

Optional featured placements and clearly labelled local ads fund the platform. Partnered Delivery fees go in full to the delivery partner.
