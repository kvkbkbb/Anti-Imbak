{{ icon('users') }} About Anti-Imbak

Useful things deserve another chapter.

Anti-Imbak is a donation-only online marketplace that connects unused but useful items with people and communities who can benefit from them.

Every listing is free. Donors share what they no longer need, Recipients explain what would help, and both sides arrange a safe handover.

{{ logo(tagline=True) }} A marketplace where generosity is the product.
{{ icon('compass') }} Vision

A community where useful things never go to waste.

We imagine neighbourhoods where giving and receiving are ordinary, dignified and easy — and where a useful item always finds its next person.

Long term, Anti-Imbak aims to make reuse a natural first choice, reduce unnecessary waste, and strengthen local connections through generous, direct handovers.

{{ icon('flag') }} Mission

Make giving and receiving simple, safe and free.

Anti-Imbak gives every useful item a second purpose by connecting people who no longer need something with people who do.

  • {{ icon('check') }} Keep every donated item free — no prices, bidding or pressure to buy.
  • {{ icon('check') }} Make browsing, claiming and handovers clear, respectful and easy to follow.
  • {{ icon('check') }} Protect members with honest listings, private contact details and safety reminders.
