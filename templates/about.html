{{ icon('compass') }} Why we exist

Make sharing feel
as natural as buying.

Many homes hold things that are no longer needed but are still entirely useful. At the same time, someone nearby may need exactly that thing and cannot justify buying it new.

Anti-Imbak closes that gap with a marketplace that is intentionally different: there are no prices on donated items, no bidding, and no pressure to turn generosity into a transaction. Instead, Donors share the item's story, Recipients explain why it matters, and both sides coordinate a safe handover.

We combine the convenience of a modern marketplace with the purpose of a community-support initiative. Our work is guided by sustainability, generosity, community, innovation, accessibility and responsible consumption.

{{ icon('wallet') }} Sustainably funded

Free for people.
Supported by optional services.

The donated items themselves never generate a fee. Anti-Imbak sustains the platform through optional listing boosts and clearly labelled local advertisements. Partnered Delivery is charged as one inclusive fee and passed in full to the delivery partner.

Delivery fees stay between ₱50 and ₱150, depending on item size and distance. The donated item itself stays free.
